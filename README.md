A tModLoader 1.4 port of an unfinished, experimental Terraria mod abandoned since 2021.
Formerly (2021): A mod that should teach how important proper software design and an understanding of game design is in game development.
</h3>

Important note: Everything here labeled as "RS:" or "Retrospective" has been added recently (2026), reflecting back on stuff from 5 years ago.

<h1>
Frequently Asked Questions (2021)
</h1>
<h2>
  Why restart development?
</h2> 
I almost didn't know anything about software design when starting on this mod. I knew that Terraria's code was spaghetti, but I thought it was just its duplicated code. Now that I've finished a few books on software design over the past few weeks, I can't even bear to look at this project's code. Nothing followed the pillars of object-oriented programming. Classes were heavily coupled and were stored in the same file. Methods were extraordinarily long and in the wrong classes. I knew this project needed a restart.
<h2>
  Won't having to restart decrease your drive to continue the mod?
</h2>
Nope. I've learned from my mistakes, and the learning process never stops.
Retrospective: Oh, absolutely.
